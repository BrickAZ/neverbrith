[h1]地下室里，又多了一些不太让人放心的东西。[/h1]

《未生》是一款以道具为主的内容模组，加入了 [b]40+ 件新道具[/b]。

换个房间，连自己的角色都变了；跟了你一路的小跟班，可以拿去献祭换属性；至于那只八音盒，它能保你20秒无敌，也会在倒计时结束时要了你的命。

如果你喜欢试新道具、凑奇怪的组合，或者想给一局熟悉的以撒添点变数，欢迎来玩。

[h2]先看几件？[/h2]
[list]
[*] [b]记忆紊乱[/b]：从下一间房开始，每次进房都会随机变成一名已解锁角色，带上对方的初始能力继续打。你原来攒的构筑还在。
[*] [b]生死一念间[/b]：让本局的敌人和Boss变得更难对付，换取每层击败楼层Boss后的一张死亡证明。
[*] [b]剪碎的塔罗[/b]：把地上的三张卡牌，换成一个宝箱房道具。要留着救命，还是拿去赌一件好东西？
[*] [b]未剪断的脐带[/b]：有机会把这次受伤拖到以后。靠接下来连续两个房间无伤，争取减轻代价；再挨一下，欠的伤害立刻找上门。
[/list]

还有铜钱剑、黑太岁、鸿运齐天蛊、大狗叫……剩下的，进地下室慢慢捡。

[h1]English[/h1]

[b]A few more questionable things have found their way into the basement.[/b]

neverbrith adds [b]40+ new items[/b] to The Binding of Isaac.

Walk into the next room as a different character. Sacrifice a familiar for permanent stats. Wind up a music box that makes you invincible for 20 seconds, then kills you when the timer runs out.

If you enjoy trying new items, putting together strange builds, or giving a familiar run an unexpected turn, give it a go.

[h2]A few things you might find[/h2]
[list]
[*] [b]Memory Disorder[/b]: Starting with the next room, each room turns you into a random unlocked character with their starting abilities. Your existing build stays with you.
[*] [b]Between Life and Death[/b]: Tougher enemies and bosses for the rest of the run. In return, defeating a floor boss rewards you with Death Certificate, once per floor.
[*] [b]Shredded Tarot[/b]: Turn three card pickups into a Treasure Room item. Keep those cards for later, or trade them for something new?
[*] [b]Uncut Cord[/b]: Some hits can wait. Clear two enemy rooms without getting hit again to reduce what you owe. Take another hit, and the full amount comes due immediately.
[/list]

You might also find a Coin-Sewn Sword, Black Taisui, Fortune Rivalling Heaven Gu, or Big Dog Bark. There's more waiting in the item pools.

[h2]Version support and maintenance[/h2]
This mod is developed against [b]REPENTOGON 1.0.12a[/b]. It is expected to be compatible in theory with [b]the newer REPENTOGON builds for Repentance+[/b].

[b]Until the full release of Repentance+, no adjustments or fixes will be made in response to gameplay issues, bug reports, or other feedback specific to Repentance+.[/b]

[h2]Before you play[/h2]
[list]
[*] [b]REPENTOGON is required.[/b] Use a release compatible with your game version. See the [url=https://repentogon.com/install.html]official installation guide[/url].
[*] [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=836319872]External Item Descriptions (EID)[/url] is recommended for the mod's Chinese and English item descriptions.
[*] Restart the game after subscribing and enabling the mod.
[/list]
