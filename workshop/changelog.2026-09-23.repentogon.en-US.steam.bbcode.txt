[h1]REPENTOGON Warnings and Drop Protection[/h1]
[i]2026-09-23[/i]

[list]
[*] Improved warnings and protection for the required REPENTOGON dependency. The minimum requirement remains 1.0.12a or a newer recognized stable release.
[*] If REPENTOGON is missing, outdated, or incompatible, all mod gameplay initialization stops. A persistent warning above players in each floor's starting room asks them to install or update REPENTOGON and restart the game.
[*] Warnings follow the game's Chinese or English language setting and appear above every player in co-op. They disappear outside the starting room and reappear when players return.
[*] Only when the dependency check fails, the mod's collectibles and slot-seal trinket are removed from item pools. Mod collectibles that still appear through direct spawning, continued saves, or rerolls are replaced with Breakfast. Slot-seal trinkets become Paper Clip while retaining golden status, and Wind Charge Potion resolves to the vanilla Bad Gas effect.
[*] Items already held in saved runs are not removed. Vanilla items and other mods' drops are unaffected by this blocking.
[/list]
[i]Compatibility note: To retain 1.0.12a support, the missing-dependency protection disables gameplay and drops; the engine still reads the mod's XML and resource registrations.[/i]
