# V3 手臂叠加层提示词

内置 `image_gen.imagegen`，`transparent_background=true`。原版以撒PNG只作风格参照。输出原图以原字节保存在 `source-images/effect_arms.png`，副本为游戏侧 `effect_arms.png`。脚本只读取Alpha、声明裁切并渲染ANM2，不重画PNG。

```text
Create ONE transparent RGBA pixel-art spritesheet containing ONLY isolated magical effect arms and hands for a Binding of Isaac mod. The attached vanilla Isaac sheet is STYLE AND SCALE REFERENCE ONLY: DO NOT DRAW ISAAC, any head, face, torso, body, legs or any full character anywhere. Eight isolated RIGHT arms arranged as 4 columns and 2 rows with huge transparent spacing, NO text, NO labels, NO cell borders. Each arm floats independently, tapering to 2 wispy energy strokes at its LEFT shoulder root and ending with a clear small hand to the RIGHT; the root is a soft energy taper, no cut flesh. They look like temporary spectral extensions of tiny Isaac, NOT muscular anatomy, not a new humanoid. Small soft rounded fist, stubby simplified fingers, slender smooth forearm, chunky crisp game pixels with a 1-pixel deep blue-grey outline. Limited palette: luminous pale blue-white center, pale cyan edge, medium translucent cyan shadow, gently glowing tips. No realistic shading, no smooth airbrush glow, no sharp spikes, no lightning circles. Semi-transparent interior and true alpha-zero surrounding each arm. Eight poses, left to right top row then bottom row: (1) bent compact arm with a cupped palm for gathering an energy ball; (2) straight arm with open upright forward-pushing palm; (3) short bent arm with clenched guard fist; (4) straight extended arm with closed punching fist, oriented horizontally right so the animation can rotate it upward for uppercut; (5) bent forearm with a clearly curved gripping hand, open C shape; (6) closed curved clamp/grasp arm hugging around an invisible cylindrical target; (7) an arm extending diagonally upward-right ending in a raised open hand for super intro; (8) flexed bent-elbow energy arm with a small fist. Every pose ONE ARM ONLY. Consistent hand size and line weight across all poses. Logical sprite size roughly 26x18 pixels per arm, longer raised pose can use 26x26; render with large hard-edged pixel blocks suitable for nearest-neighbor reduction. Preserve the root-to-fist direction; no body parts besides the eight arms, no floor, no colored backing. This atlas is an OVERLAY and must leave the existing character completely untouched.
```
